# Tower Toppler '26

A 2026 reboot of the Atari 7800 classic **Tower Toppler** (*Nebulus*, Hewson 1987), built for phones first.

Eight towers are poisoning the seas of planet Nebulus. Guide Pogo up each rotating tower, reach the summit door, and topple it. Each tower you topple heals the ocean a little, until the whole sea runs clear.

## The classic, kept faithful
- Getting hit **knocks Pogo down to the ledge below**. If there's no ledge below, he falls into the sea and you lose a life.
- **Bouncing balls & molecules** pop when shot. **Silver rollers** can only be frozen. **Eyes** are invulnerable.
- **Flying nasties** come round the tower at your height. A red **!** warns you which side they're coming from.
- Crumbling, slippery, conveyor and shootable-block ledges; lifts; tunnels through the tower; a time limit on every climb.

## What's new
**Moves**
- Hold FIRE to charge a **mega-snowball** that pierces and freezes anything.
- **Frozen enemies become ice blocks** you can climb on.
- Snowballs **wrap all the way round the tower**, so they can come back and hit you.
- **Ground pound**: push down in mid-air to stun enemies and smash blocks.
- **Ledge grab**: Pogo catches a ledge lip if he falls just short.
- **Rewind**: hold ⏪ to turn back the last few seconds.

**A signature idea per tower**
1. The Tower of Eyes: the tutorial climb.
2. Realm of Robots: clockwork ledges that retract into the wall.
3. Trap of Tricks: trick doors hide secret vaults.
4. Slippery Slide: a blizzard with gusts; frozen enemies stay frozen longer.
5. Broken Path: the tower crumbles away beneath you.
6. Swimmer's Delight: the sea rises. The water you can see *is* the timer.
7. Nasty One: a night storm, with Pogo's lantern and lightning flashes.
8. Edge of Doom: gales, embers, and a **guardian eye** that seals the exit until you defeat it.

**Replay**
- Each tower has three medals: speed, no hits, and all 3 hidden ★ star-fish.
- Race the **ghost** of your own best climb.
- **Daily Tower**: a new tower every day, the same for everyone.
- **Endless**: procedurally generated towers. Every generated tower is checked to be beatable.
- Unlockable Pogo skins and hats, mirror mode, and a shareable score card.

**Between towers**
- The submarine ride takes you through the sea you just healed to the next tower. Catch fish and avoid jellyfish.

**Feel**
- Clouds drift below you and birds circle as you climb.
- The tower sways near the top, and the summit gets a slow-motion celebration before the tower falls into the sea.
- The music is different for each tower and builds in layers as you climb.
- Enemies are positioned in stereo, so you can hear them behind the tower before you see them. Wind, rain and creaks set the mood.

**Look**
- A tilt-shift "toy diorama" look, soft glow and real-time shadows, with graphics quality that adapts to your device automatically.

**Comfort & access**
- Assist mode (infinite lives and rewind), Classic rules, a game-speed setting, reduced motion, and a vibration toggle.
- Remembers your stick side, settings, medals and ghosts.

## Controls
| | Touch | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|---|
| Walk | Floating analog stick (swap sides in Settings) | ← → / A D | Left stick / D-pad |
| Doors & lifts | Push stick ▲ / ▼ | ↑ ↓ / W S | Stick / D-pad |
| Ground pound | Stick ▼ in mid-air | ↓ in mid-air | Down in mid-air |
| Jump | JUMP | Space / Z / K | A / B |
| Fire / charge / call lift | FIRE (hold to charge) | X / J / Enter | X / Y / RB / RT |
| Rewind | ⏪ REWIND (hold) | R / Backspace | LB / LT / Back |
| Pause | ⏸ | P / Esc | Start |

## Install & run
- It's an installable web app that works offline. Open it in your phone's browser, then use **Install** (or Share ▸ Add to Home Screen).
- To run it locally, serve the folder statically (for example `python3 -m http.server`) and open `index.html`. There's no build step; Three.js loads from the jsDelivr CDN.
